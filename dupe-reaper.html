Dupe Reaper

Find duplicate photos on your iPhone, keep the best shot and clear out the rest.

Coming soon to iPhone

What it does

  • Finds identical copies of a photo, plus resized and re-saved versions.
  • Shows how much space duplicates are using and how much you'll recover.
  • Suggests which photo in each group to keep.
  • Selects only identical copies on its own. You choose the rest.
  • Best Shot finds similar photos of the same moment and suggests the best one: eyes open, smiling and sharp.

Built to be careful

  • Your photos stay on your device. Dupe Reaper doesn't upload them to us.
  • It won't let you delete every photo in a group.
  • iOS asks you to confirm every delete.
  • Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for about 30 days.
